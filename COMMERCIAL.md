# Commercial licensing

Ghosttree's public terms are meant to keep personal, research, nonprofit, and
genuinely small-team use practical without giving away a hosted-service or
enterprise product.

You need a separate commercial agreement if, for example:

- your organization does not qualify for the Micro Business Grant;
- you want to host or operate Ghosttree for anyone else;
- Ghosttree functionality will be exposed to customers or embedded in a
  product;
- you want OEM, white-label, resale, sublicensing, or alternative distribution
  rights; or
- you need contractual support, warranties, indemnities, or other negotiated
  terms.

Deadweight Labs UG (haftungsbeschränkt) may offer managed hosting and related
services separately. No public hosting offer or service-level commitment is
made by this repository.

Contact [legal@deadweightlabs.com](mailto:legal@deadweightlabs.com) with a short
description of the organization, intended use, number of users, and whether
third parties will access the software.

This page is a practical summary, not a substitute for the [license](LICENSE),
the [Micro Business Grant](COMMUNITY-GRANT.md), or professional legal advice.
