# Code of conduct

Ghosttree's community spaces are for careful technical collaboration. Treat
other people with respect, disagree with ideas rather than identities, and make
room for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
and sustained personal attacks are not welcome. Neither is publishing another
person's private information or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that
undermine a safe and productive project. Serious or repeated misconduct can
lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns to
[legal@deadweightlabs.com](mailto:legal@deadweightlabs.com). Reports will be
handled as privately as practical. Anyone involved in the report will step out
of enforcement decisions where they have a conflict of interest.
