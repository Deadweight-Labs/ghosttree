Task: a pull request was merged. pr.sessions.user_messages holds what people
typed to their agents in the sessions behind its commits, session by session.
Find what they asked for that the pull request did not deliver.

A wish is a feature, a change, a complaint about behaviour or a constraint a
person stated. Not a wish: instructions for the task at hand ("run the tests",
"commit this"), questions, corrections of the agent's last answer.

Judge "delivered" by pr.text, pr.commits and pr.diff. Leave delivered wishes out
or mark them done. One proposal per wish at most, never two for the same wish.
If an open issue in requests.open already carries the wish, set same_as to its
number instead of proposing it again.

Answer:
{"wishes": [{"title": "what the person wants, one line", "body": "what was asked
  for and any condition attached", "session": "the ref of the session",
  "quote": "exact words the person typed in that session", "done": false,
  "same_as": "REQ-12 or empty"}]}

The quote must come from the messages of exactly the session you name, from a
message a person typed, never from the other inputs.
