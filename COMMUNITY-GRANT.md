## Who qualifies

Your organization qualifies while both conditions are true:

1. it has fewer than 10 staff; and
2. either its annual turnover or its annual balance-sheet total does not exceed
   EUR 2 million.

Count the staff and financial figures of organizations that control, are
controlled by, or are under common control with your organization together.
Splitting one business across related entities does not create separate grants.

This threshold follows the micro-enterprise scale used in Article 2 of the
annex to [Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32003H0361),
with the group aggregation stated above.

## What this grant permits

A qualifying organization may use and modify Ghosttree for its own operations,
including commercial work and ordinary projects carried out for clients. A
consultancy or small product team may therefore use Ghosttree while doing its
work, as long as Ghosttree itself is not the service or product supplied to the
client.

You may run Ghosttree on infrastructure you control for your own permitted use.

## What this grant does not permit

This grant does not permit you to:

- host or operate Ghosttree for third parties;
- offer Ghosttree, or substantial Ghosttree functionality, as a service;
- resell, sublicense, OEM, white-label, or distribute Ghosttree as part of a
  commercial offering; or
- give customers or other third parties access to substantial Ghosttree
  functionality.

Hosted and managed Ghosttree services are reserved to Deadweight Labs UG
(haftungsbeschränkt), unless a written commercial agreement says otherwise.

## When eligibility ends

If your organization no longer meets the qualification thresholds, this grant
ends for new use 90 days after the end of the financial year in which it ceased
to qualify. Continued commercial use then requires a separate agreement.
